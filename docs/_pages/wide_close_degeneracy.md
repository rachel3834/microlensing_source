The wide-close degeneracy occurs for binary lenses due to similarities of shape 
between the caustic structures.  This is fully explored in [Dominik (1999)](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/1999A%26A...349..108D/abstract).

As discussed in [caustics](/theory/caustics/), binary caustics structures can have 
one of three morphologies:

The single, central caustic of a close binary is similar in shape to either one 
of the two diamond-shaped structures that compose a wide binary caustic.  
As a result, it is possible for the relative lens-source trajectory to pass or 
intersect these caustics in such a way that results in very similar magnification, 
and hence lightcurve morphology.
