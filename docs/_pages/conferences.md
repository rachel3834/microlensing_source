## International Conference on Microlensing
The worldwide microlensing community organizes a conference on a roughly annual basis. 

* <a href="https://lco.global/workshops/microlensing18/">18th International Conference on Microlensing (2014, Santa Barbara, USA)</a>
* <a href="https://asd.gsfc.nasa.gov/conferences/microlensing19/">19th International Conference on Microlensing (2015, Annapolis, USA)</a>
* <a href="https://nexsci.caltech.edu/conferences/2017/microlensing/">21st International Conference on Microlensing (2017, Pasadena, USA)</a>
* <a href="https://www.simonsfoundation.org/event/23rd-international-microlensing-conference/">Recordings of the 23rd International Microlensing Conference (2019, New York, USA)</a>
* <a href="https://www.cold-worlds.com/nouvelles-scientifiques/workshops-2022/">25th International Microlensing Conference (2022, Paris, France)</a>
* <a href="https://space-science.llnl.gov/microlensing-conference-26">26th International Microlensing Conference (2024, Livermore, USA)</a>
* <a href="https://microlensing2025.saao.ac.za/">27th International Microlensing Conference (2025, Cape Town, South Africa)</a>
* <a href="https://microlensing2026.de/">28th International Microlensing Conference (2024, Heidelberg, Germany)</a>

## Sagan Workshops
NASA's annual Sagan Workshop is aimed at graduate students and post-docs and provides 
detailed training on a field related to exoplanet detection and characterization.  
The microlensing community has organized this workshop twice. 

* <a href="https://nexsci.caltech.edu/workshop/2026/">2026 Sagan Exoplanet Summery Workshop</a>
* <a href="https://nexsci.caltech.edu/workshop/2017/">2017 Sagan Exoplanet Summer Workshop</a>
    * <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QPfKucBb9B8&list=PLIbTYGsIVYthWRS14eCEK8SK9IOTcaYsf">Recordings of 2017 Sagan Exoplanet Summer Workshop</a>
