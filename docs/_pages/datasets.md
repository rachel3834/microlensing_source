There are a number of real and simulated datasets including microlensing events, 
which are useful both to explore and for software testing. 

## Real datasets
The [NASA Exoplanet Archive](https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/data.html) 
hosts a number of datasets relevant to microlensing.  The lightcurves for planetary 
events are available from the planets table, and the following survey data releases  
are also available. 

* [DREAMS microlensing survey](https://astro.westlake.edu.cn/dreams/)
* KMTNet survey data from K2 campaign See [Kim et al.(2017)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1703.06883.pdf) and [Kim et al.(2018)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1801.08166.pdf)
* [UKIRT microlensing survey](https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/cgi-bin/TblSearch/nph-tblSearchInit?app=ExoTbls&config=ukirttimeseries) Described in [Shvartzvald et al.(2018)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1610.02039.pdf)
* The VISTA Variables in the Via Lactea (VVV) Survey in near infra-red passbands publicly released ([Minniti et al. 2010](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0912.1056.pdf)) aperture photometry lightcurve data for all sources between 2010 and 2015.
  * [VVV Survey data](http://archive.eso.org/cms/eso-archive-news/New_Data_Release_of_VVV_Photometric_Catalogues_via_the_ESO_Science_Archive_Facility.html)
* [MOA microlensing survey](https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/cgi-bin/MOA/nph-firefly?MOA)
  * The lightcurves of 474 microlensing events were published by [Sumi et al.(2013)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1305.0186.pdf)
* [OGLE microlensing survey data archive](http://ogledb.astrouw.edu.pl/~ogle/photdb/)
  * 214 OGLE-II/III lightcurves were published in [Udalski et al.(2000)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0807.3884.pdf)
  * 3718 OGLE-III lightcurves were published by [Wyrzykowski et al.(2015)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1405.3134.pdf)
* [ROME/REA microlensing survey](https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/cgi-bin/ROME/nph-firefly?data&filter=True)
* [K2 Campaign 9](https://archive.stsci.edu/k2/)
* The lightcurves of 564 microlensing events from the MACHO Bulge survey were published in [Thomas et al.(2005)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/astro-ph/0410341.pdf)

In addition many of the ground-based surveys provide reduced data products 
for events discovered in real-time.  See the [Ground-based Surveys]({{ '/observations/ground-based-surveys/' | relative_url }}) for 
more information.
