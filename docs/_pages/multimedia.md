There are a range of sources of high quality graphics and movies relating to microlensing 
available.  Follow the links below to explore.  

## NASA
[NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/) have created 
a wealth of high quality graphics relating to microlensing, particularly in 
support of the Roman Mission.

## RGES-PIT
The [Roman Project Investigation Team](https://rges-pit.org/outreach/) have 
produced a number of outreach resources linked from the team website. 

## Images and movies from this website
